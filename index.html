Hi there!,

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Politics at Princeton University. Currently, I am visiting the MZES Mannheim as a doctoral guest researchers. In my dissertation, I investigate how strong local party organization are built and what consequences they have. I hold a B.A. in International Economics from the University of Tübingen and a M.A. in Economics from Humboldt University in Berlin.

My research has been published or is forthcoming in the American Political Science Review, American Journal of Political Science and Public Opinion Quarterly.
