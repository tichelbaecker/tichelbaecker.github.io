Political Elites & Party Organizations

Who becomes a politician and why? I study how parties select candidates, how voter discrimination shapes representation, and how new parties build local organizations.

Polarization

How do citizens perceive political divides? I examine the measurement and causes of affective polarization across countries and how people understand ideological dimensions.

Challenges to Liberal Democracy

How do war, violence, and nationalism undermine democratic politics? I study how exposure to conflict fuels support for extremist parties and affects political participation.
